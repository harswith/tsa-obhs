About Us

What is TSA?

The Technology Student Association (TSA) is a national, non-profit organization of more than 300,000 middle and high school students engaged in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM). Almost 5,000,000 student members have participated in TSA since 1978 through challenging competitions, leadership opportunities, and community service.

The Journey

How Competition Works

1

Chapter Level

Members prepare and compete in their chosen events, building skills and refining projects throughout the school year.

2

State Conference

Because there are only a small number of TSA chapters in the Columbus, Ohio area, TSA OBHS competes directly at the State level, there is no regional round. State conference details are TBA and will be announced when released.

3

National Conference

Top-placing competitors at States qualify for Nationals based on that year's placement guidelines for each event.
